1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how 3D Live Wallpapers: 4K Parallax ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the application. We respect your privacy and aim to explain what information may be processed in connection with wallpaper browsing, previews, customization, and applying wallpapers.

By using the app, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

2. Information and permissions

Depending on the app version, features you use, and your device settings, the app may process the following:

  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, diagnostics, and performance information where collected.
  • Usage information: interactions with wallpaper categories, previews, settings, and app features where analytics tools are enabled.
  • Wallpaper content: wallpaper images, videos, or visual assets requested for display or download within the app.
  • Device features and permissions: Android wallpaper services and any storage, media, or other permissions requested by a specific feature. The app should only access such permissions when needed for that feature.
  • Support messages: information you choose to provide when contacting us by email.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to display wallpaper collections, load previews and wallpaper assets, enable wallpaper customization and application, maintain app functionality, improve performance, diagnose errors, understand feature usage, and respond to support requests.

4. Storage and wallpaper content

Wallpaper assets or user-selected settings may be cached or stored locally on your device to support app features. The exact storage behavior depends on the app version and features used. Applying a wallpaper may use Android's system wallpaper functionality.

5. Third-party services

The app may use third-party services such as content delivery or hosting providers, analytics tools, crash-reporting services, and advertising partners. These providers may process technical information or device identifiers as necessary to provide their services. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Advertising and analytics

If advertising or analytics services are included in a particular app version, those providers may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and device information, subject to applicable law and the consent choices presented to you. You can manage available consent choices through the app or your device settings where supported.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers supporting the app, disclosed when required by law, or used to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the service.

8. Data retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Your choices and rights

You can manage app permissions through your Android settings and may uninstall the app at any time. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Contact us to submit a privacy request.

10. Children's privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the request.

11. International data transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable, appropriate safeguards may be used for such transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be made available with an updated date. Continued use of the app after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
